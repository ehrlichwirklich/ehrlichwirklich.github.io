I love to create and find great joy in bringing an idea to life. While I have struggled to recognize my own work as qualifying me to be an *artist* in the traditional sense, I am coming to accept that there is no lower bound to creative tasks and producing art. In that vein, this section will feature everything from handicrafts to my efforts in learning to draw. I hope to see not only a progression of my work, but also be able to identify the forces that influenece me over time.
